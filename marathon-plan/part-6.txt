      <h2>Workout and exercise guide</h2>
      <p>This section explains the running terms used throughout the program so every workout can be followed correctly.</p>

      <div class="grid-2">
        <div class="mini-card">
          <span class="pill green">Easy run</span>
          <p>A relaxed run at conversational effort.</p>
          <ul>
            <li><strong>Effort:</strong> 3 to 4 out of 10</li>
            <li><strong>Breathing:</strong> calm enough to speak in full sentences</li>
            <li><strong>Purpose:</strong> builds endurance and supports recovery</li>
          </ul>
          <p>Do not chase pace. The runner should finish feeling comfortable.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="mini-card">
          <span class="pill blue">Steady run</span>
          <p>Faster than easy pace, but still controlled.</p>
          <ul>
            <li><strong>Effort:</strong> 5 to 6 out of 10</li>
            <li><strong>Breathing:</strong> deeper, but still controlled</li>
            <li><strong>Purpose:</strong> improves aerobic strength</li>
          </ul>
          <p><strong>Example:</strong> 4 km easy, 3 km steady, 2 km easy.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="mini-card">
          <span class="pill orange">Strides</span>
          <p>Short, fast and relaxed accelerations completed after an easy run.</p>
          <p><strong>Example: 6 × 20-second strides</strong></p>
          <ol>
            <li>Accelerate smoothly for the first 5 seconds.</li>
            <li>Run fast and relaxed for about 10 seconds.</li>
            <li>Slow down gradually.</li>
            <li>Walk or jog for 60 to 90 seconds.</li>
          </ol>
          <p>Use about 85 to 90% effort. Strides are not full sprints.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="mini-card">
          <span class="pill orange">Flat sprints</span>
          <p>Short, powerful runs on flat ground.</p>
          <ul>
            <li>Warm up for at least 15 minutes.</li>
            <li>Run fast for 15 to 20 seconds.</li>
            <li>Walk or jog for around 90 seconds.</li>
            <li>Repeat while form remains clean.</li>
          </ul>
          <p>These should be fast and smooth, not maximum-effort races.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="mini-card">
          <span class="pill orange">Hill sprints</span>
          <p>Very short, powerful runs uphill on a moderate slope.</p>
          <p><strong>Example: 6 × 10-second hill sprints</strong></p>
          <ol>
            <li>Run strongly uphill for 10 to 12 seconds.</li>
