      <h2>Weekly strength session</h2>
      <div class="mini-card">
        <span class="pill blue">Monday strength</span>
        <p>Complete this once per week. Rest 60 to 90 seconds between sets. Choose weights that leave around two clean repetitions in reserve.</p>
        <div class="grid-2">
          <ul>
            <li>Goblet squat or barbell squat: 3 × 8</li>
            <li>Romanian deadlift: 3 × 8</li>
            <li>Bulgarian split squat: 3 × 8 each leg</li>
            <li>Standing calf raise: 3 × 12 to 15</li>
          </ul>
          <ul>
            <li>Bent-knee calf raise: 3 × 12 to 15</li>
            <li>Hamstring curl: 3 × 10 to 12</li>
            <li>Plank: 3 × 40 to 60 sec</li>
            <li>Side plank: 3 × 30 to 45 sec each side</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </div>
      <p>In recovery and peak weeks, reduce the session to two sets. Never train to failure during marathon preparation.</p>
    </section>
